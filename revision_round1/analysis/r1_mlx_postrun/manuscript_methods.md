# Manuscript methods subsection (working draft)

We selected the frozen 300-question answerable cohort identified by cohort hash `bf4cc128d85d5534e28a5a0804bef0c6eb6ddfed44a1777fc1ad42d050cf7074` from the October-2026 database snapshot `external_db-20261016T193200Z`. Each question was prepared in two configuration-specific arms: structured context ON and OFF. The ON arm inserted only the eligible static graph-relation block; retrieved text fragments, question, dynamic graph enrichment, evidence identifiers, formatting, retrieval budget, model, tokenizer, and decoding were held constant. The final audit observed 278/300 activation-positive interventions and 22 activation-negative controls.

Answers were generated with local `occ-ai/OCC-RAG-1.7B` using MLX (`mlx=0.31.2`, `mlx_lm=0.31.3`), greedy decoding, temperature 0, and a 768-token maximum. Predictive telemetry followed `r1-mlx-predictive-distribution-v1` (`mlx_lm.generate_step@0.31.3`): H20, H50, and H100 are entropy in bits after local renormalization of the selected top-20, top-50, and top-100 predictive probabilities, averaged over compact generated-token rows including the stop row. This is distinct from the unavailable legacy full-distribution entropy field.

The durable ledger and accepted SHA-256 hashes were checked before analysis. Failed generations were retained in the denominator; in this completed v2 ledger both arms had 300/300 successful outputs. Primary inference used query-level ON/OFF pairing and a fixed-seed (20261113) nonparametric bootstrap with 10000 resamples. No R1 semantic judge had been executed at audit time; quality analyses are therefore pending rather than imputed.
