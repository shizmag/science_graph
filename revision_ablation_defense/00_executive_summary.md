# Executive summary — CUDA ablation evidence audit

**Manuscript:** *When Lower Generation Entropy Meets Lower Answer Quality: Comparative Telemetry Across Biomedical RAG Configurations*
**Journal / ID:** Knowledge-Based Systems / KNOSYS-D-26-22285
**Audit status:** `READY_WITH_EXPLICIT_LIMITATIONS` for an evidence-based reviewer response, subject to author confirmation and external decision-letter verification. No manuscript or prior response/supplement files were modified.
**Implementation:** internal B6 full is the paper-facing B5 arm. Historical July MLX B3–B5 comparisons are separate from this new CUDA component-sensitivity run.

## Decision-level findings

1. **Accounting is internally consistent:** the frozen run contains 375 unique queries × 14 arms = 5,250 planned query-arm records. Independently reconstructed records show 4,265 successful generations and 985 failed generations. All 985 failures are nontransient serialized-prompt context-cap errors (12,000-token configured context cap); failed responses have no generation and no judge score. All 4,265 eligible answers have one score record for each of the four judge metrics.
2. **Judge duplicate reuse is defensible at the evaluation-payload level:** 2,297 unique exact cases were judged; 1,968 duplicate response slots reused scores. Reuse identity includes question, answerability label, gold reference, actual evaluator-visible context, and cleaned answer. Same-payload duplicate score tuples agree; response hashes and case IDs reconcile. The global judge rubric/model/decode settings were constant for this single run, but were not embedded in each per-case hash. No cross-run score cache is present. We did not independently replay each remote batch; the identity audit supports exact-input reuse, not construct validity of an LLM judge.
3. **Parent deduplication is the strongest quality signal.** Among 296 query IDs with successful B6 and no-parent-dedup outputs, ablation-minus-B6 Semantic Accuracy is +12.47 pp (paired percentile bootstrap 95% CI +7.88 to +16.97 pp). Exact two-sided paired sign-test p=2.09e-6; Holm p=2.71e-5 within the 13 Semantic Accuracy arm contrasts and 1.08e-4 over all 52 arm×metric contrasts. This is exploratory/post-hoc and conditional on jointly successful outputs; the single LLM judge is not a gold-standard clinical assessor. The direction is not explained by differential pair composition in the paired estimate, but does not automatically generalize to the 79 B6 failures.
4. **The GraphExpander effect is a trade-off, not a uniformly positive or negative component effect.** On the 296 jointly generated query pairs, disabling it changes Semantic Accuracy +7.44 pp (95% CI +2.22 to +12.77), but loses significance after Holm correction (p=.361 in the Semantic Accuracy family; p=1.0 across 52). Faithfulness improves +7.33 pp (95% CI +2.29 to +12.11; all-52 Holm p=.022), while Citation Fidelity worsens −12.80 pp (95% CI −18.84 to −6.73; all-52 Holm p=.00194). Answer Relevance is +2.82 pp with interval crossing zero.
5. **The GraphExpander result is strongly entangled with availability.** Full B6 succeeds on 296/375 (78.9%), versus 375/375 with expansion disabled. All B6 failures overflow; none of the disabled-expander queries fail. Thus the paired quality contrast alone is a common-success estimand, while the all-query service-utility contrast incorporates availability as a separately defined policy penalty. This is not an MCAR claim. Extreme no-imputation score-scale bounds for all-query potential Semantic Accuracy are +1.93 to +22.99 pp for no-expander and +0.27 to +28.80 pp for no-parent-dedup, under each unobserved potential score allowed anywhere in [0,1]. Those wide bounds do not support precision for the population effect.
6. **Answerability subgroups reveal a GraphExpander trade-off.** On the same jointly successful cases: answerable Semantic Accuracy is +10.79 pp (N=248, 95% CI +5.24 to +16.27); designated-negative Semantic Accuracy is −9.90 pp (N=48, 95% CI −25.00 to +4.58). These 75 negatives are synthetic designated negatives, not independently human-validated corpus-wide unanswerables. Parser UNKNOWN is not abstention, and failed generation is neither an abstention nor a quality score.
7. **Parent deduplication changes evidence concentration, and the chunk metric inflates its apparent gain.** On paired successes, the no-dedup arm retains five selected chunks but averages 1.97 unique PMIDs vs 4.75 for B6; it averages 3.86 gold-paper chunks vs 0.94. Chunk-level context precision rises by 56.0 pp, but source-level/document-deduplicated AP changes 0.0 pp on these PubMedQA single-gold-PMID items. Thus the chunk precision rise mostly counts repeated chunks from the sole relevant PMID, not more unique source coverage. This is consistent with a plausible evidence-concentration mechanism; it does not prove repeated chunks caused better judged answers.
8. **Several nominal interventions were execution no-ops for answer generation:** B6 minus static graph context, and fixed-budget context, have identical serialized prompts and raw responses for all 296 B6-success cases (all four judge metrics tie exactly). The static context flag is false in the effective config but trace shows static context was not inserted in the selected prompt branch. Fixed-budget policy executed, yet serialized prompts/responses stayed identical. No independent generation quality effect is estimable there. Disabling concept retrieval changed branch execution but only one of 296 prompts/responses; intrapaper candidate disabling changed 24 prompts/responses among 294 shared outputs; treat effects as small/cohort-specific, not proof of general absence.
9. **Entropy and quality can move together in both directions, without a universal monotonic rule.** On matched pairs, no-parent-dedup yields higher H20/H50/H100 (+.0430/.0453/.0461 bits) and higher Semantic Accuracy (+12.47 pp); no-expander yields lower H20/H50/H100 (−.0548/−.0573/−.0579 bits) and higher Semantic Accuracy (+7.44 pp, unadjusted only). Retained top-K mass changes are near zero (about −0.00023 to +0.000045 for parent-off and +0.000204 to −0.000114 for no-expander across K=20/50/100). These are descriptive co-movements, not causal evidence or a calibrated entropy-quality law. The entropy metric is mean over generated answer tokens, top-K locally renormalized entropy, not full-vocabulary Shannon entropy.

## Reviewer-ready core sentence

“We conducted an additional post-review, same-runtime CUDA component-sensitivity analysis of the B5-equivalent B6 reference against 13 variants on a frozen 375-query set. The principal matched-success result is that removing parent-chunk deduplication was associated with higher judge-assigned Semantic Accuracy (+12.47 pp, N=296, query-paired 95% CI +7.88 to +16.97; Holm-adjusted p=.000108 across all 52 arm-by-metric contrasts). Disabling dynamic graph expansion showed a smaller, unadjusted Semantic Accuracy increase (+7.44 pp) but a robust Citation Fidelity decrease (−12.80 pp), and effects were directionally different for answerable versus designated-negative queries. The analysis identifies plausible context-construction contributions but does not decompose the historical July MLX B3–B5 difference into causal component effects.”
